Kazakh (kk_KZ) Hunspell dictionary
Source: https://github.com/kergalym/myspell-kk

Developed under the OpenOffice:Lingucomponent:Spell Checking/Dictionaries
subproject. Word list amended with Kazakh names created for Aspell by
Alexey Lipchansky. Affix file developed by Akmaral Mussayeva based on
appendixes to the dictionary of Kaldybai Bektaiuly. Based on the word
list of aspell-kk_KZ-0.2, containing over 53 thousand main words.
